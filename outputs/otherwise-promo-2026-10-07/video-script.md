# OtherWise 宣传视频配音与分镜

面向可能提供指导的教授与研究者。通过日常的重复推荐体验、相关研究和真实产品演示，提出 OtherWise 的研究问题：由用户主导的跨领域探索，是否能帮助一次新发现发展为持续兴趣。建议成片约 2 分 45 秒。

## 配音与画面节奏

英文配音约 291 词。时间表是剪辑参考，总计 2 分 46 秒；先生成完整配音，再按实际句长调整镜头。研究卡片和片尾可以保留短暂阅读停顿。字幕文件未加时间码，待最终音轨确定后对齐。

配音采用自然、清晰、带好奇心的讲述语气，产品介绍时稍微明亮。方括号仅用于 Eleven v3 或 v4 的音频方向，正式字幕使用无标签文件。[ElevenLabs 官方提示指南](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/capabilities/text-to-speech/best-practices)。

## 画面风格

建议横版 16:9，1920 × 1080，30 fps。真实界面尽量以更高分辨率录制，剪辑时只做适度裁切和推近。主色沿用产品：深蓝 #101a29、鼠尾草绿 #bccfc5、浅米白 #f0ecdf，少量雾蓝作点缀。

画面以静止构图、缓慢推近、细线和柔和亮点为主。产品操作用真实录屏，抽象开场和过渡可用 AI。所有品牌字标、论文标题、数字和二维码在剪辑软件中添加。AI 不需要生成可阅读文字。

1080p 成片的主标题可用 56–68 px，字幕 40–48 px，研究出处 26–30 px；字幕最多两行，底部保留约 90 px 安全边距。研究出处放在画面上方，避免与英文字幕重叠。标题字体可沿用产品的衬线风格，正文和字幕用清晰的无衬线字体。

背景音乐选择无歌词、缓慢展开的轻环境音乐，音量让位于人声。开头卡片可加轻微滑动声，产品点击处加很轻的确认声，研究结果出现时保留干净的听觉空间。

```text
[curious] You open your feed for something new.
[short pause] Somehow, you keep returning to the same topics.

The videos change.
The interests we explore can stay surprisingly familiar.
We began to worry that our interests were narrowing without us noticing.

[thoughtful] Research gave us a reason to take that question seriously.
A Spotify study linked algorithmic listening with lower consumption diversity.

Other researchers showed, in simulations, how recommendation feedback loops can make users' choices more similar and reduce the value they get.

That raised a question for us:
How can we make unfamiliar interests easier to discover?

[short pause] This is OtherWise.
Other, for another perspective.
Wise, for more thoughtful judgment.
And an invitation to think otherwise.

[excited] Start with an interest you choose.
OtherWise suggests unfamiliar but related topics across fields.

You control how far to explore, from nearby ideas to broader connections.

Follow a topic that catches your attention.
Save it when you want to pursue it, and explore from that new starting point.

A galaxy map makes your exploration visible, so you can revisit discoveries and choose where to go next.

[thoughtful] Existing research gives this direction an initial basis.
In a small user study, Auralist produced more useful, previously unknown recommendations.
Its authors also proposed letting users adjust the level of novelty.

PURS showed how unexpected recommendations could remain relevant.

Our proposed contribution is to bring these ideas into a general topic explorer:
interests you confirm, gradual exploration across fields, and a visual record of your journey.

We're developing it as both a product and a research project.
Next, we want to test whether a new discovery today can grow into a lasting interest.

If your research connects with recommendation, learning, or human-computer interaction, we'd love your guidance.

[short pause] OtherWise.
Your world, a little wider.
```

### 镜头 03 Spotify 的观察证据

### 镜头 04 推荐反馈的机制

参考时间 00:31–00:43。

**配音**

**画面**

三张卡片依次连接成环：Recommended content → User choices → Training data → Recommended content。再出现几个逐渐相似的选择点。

**屏幕文字**

Recommendation feedback loops

Simulation study · Chaney et al., 2018

**制作与转场**

剪辑软件用形状和箭头制作。最后强调 More similar choices / Lower utility。标明模拟研究。来源：[Chaney et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1710.11214)。

## AI 生成素材的 prompts

以下均为抽象概念画面。每段可单独复制使用。能使用参考帧时，以 P2 末帧衔接 P3，统一星点、配色和亮度。只有图片生成工具时，先生成对应静帧，再用剪辑软件做 3–5% 慢推、平移和遮罩；这些镜头不依赖复杂物体运动。

### P1 重复出现的内容卡片

对应镜头 01，生成长度建议 8 秒。

```text
Create an 8-second silent 16:9 cinematic editorial animation. Against a matte midnight navy background (#101a29), a neat vertical column of floating image tiles scrolls upward at a steady pace. The tiles contain variations of the same botanical subject: leaves, small gardens, potted plants, and soil. Each image is different, while the recurring visual motifs feel increasingly familiar. Use restrained sage (#bccfc5), muted steel blue, and soft ivory accents. Gentle studio lighting, crisp edges, stable geometry, subtle camera push-in. Keep the bottom 20 percent uncluttered for subtitles. No readable text, logos, recognizable app interfaces, people, hands, watermarks, flicker, or distorted tiles.
```

### P2 熟悉兴趣之外仍有未探索的领域

对应镜头 02，生成长度建议 8 秒，可将静帧慢推补足镜头。

```text
Create an 8-second silent 16:9 abstract editorial animation about unexplored interests. A small cluster of softly glowing sage points occupies the center of a spacious midnight navy field (#101a29). Several delicate lines connect only the nearby points. The camera slowly pulls back, revealing other distinct clusters in muted steel blue and pale ivory at the edges. The central cluster remains the same size in the scene; the wider view reveals additional possibilities. Calm, thoughtful, precise, sophisticated scientific illustration, soft restrained glow, stable point positions. Keep the lower 20 percent clear for subtitles. No words, numbers, charts, brains, faces, UI, logos, dramatic explosions, flicker, or watermarks.
```

### P3 从熟悉主题走向一个新起点

对应镜头 05，可复用为镜头 13 的背景，生成长度建议 7 秒。

```text
Create a 7-second silent 16:9 cinematic abstract transition. On a matte midnight navy field (#101a29), a small connected cluster of sage points sits on the left and a second quieter cluster sits on the right. One warm ivory point follows a single graceful curved path toward the second cluster. As it arrives, two nearby points gently brighten, suggesting another starting point for exploration. The surrounding clusters remain stable. Very slow camera drift, clean composition, restrained sage and steel blue palette, subtle soft glow, generous negative space, elegant editorial motion design. Leave the bottom 20 percent clear. No readable text, numbers, logos, UI, rocket imagery, explosions, rapid zooms, morphing geometry, or watermarks.
```

### P4 品牌片尾背景

对应镜头 16，也可作片尾邀请的背景，生成长度建议 8 秒，成片裁切为 6 秒。

```text
Create an 8-second silent 16:9 background plate for a research project end card. A deep midnight navy field (#101a29) contains sparse delicate star-like points in pale ivory, muted steel blue, and sage. A few points gradually become visible near the outer edges, subtly widening the composition. Keep the central 60 percent quiet and nearly empty for a wordmark added later, and keep the bottom 20 percent clear. Almost static camera, premium understated editorial design, subtle film texture, soft controlled glow, no busy nebula. Do not generate any text, lettering, logo, QR code, people, interface, lens flare, rapid particle motion, or watermark.
```

## 录屏素材清单

录制时使用英文 UI 和干净的演示资料，手动添加 Gardening。先实际获取一次推荐，选定一个适合继续探索的主题，整条演示沿用它。推荐内容由运行结果决定，不预先指定论文或系统并未返回的主题。

1. Interests：手动添加 Gardening，停在已保存兴趣状态。
2. Discover：Find ideas、真实返回列表，以及一张卡片的关联说明。
3. Settings：只录探索范围控件和保存动作，然后回到 Discover 获取另一批结果。当前界面用现有数值控件演示。
4. Discover：同一主题的搜索动作、Save interest，以及以新保存主题继续 Follow a path。
5. Map：真实探索记录、选择主题、Explore from here 或 Focus，以及能清楚看见主题关系的全屏镜头。
6. 三段界面静帧：已保存的兴趣、范围控件和地图，用于镜头 13。

每段操作前后各多录 2–3 秒，剪辑时更容易安排配音和阅读停顿。先把模型与页面加载完成，再录主操作；较长等待可缩短，保持操作状态和主题连续。

## 论文与研究画面的文字

**镜头 03**

Algorithmic Effects on the Diversity of Consumption on Spotify

Anderson et al. · The Web Conference 2020

Algorithmic listening ↔ lower consumption diversity

Observed association

[论文](https://doi.org/10.1145/3366423.3380281)。该结果支持算法驱动听歌与较低消费多样性的关联，不直接测量心理兴趣面变化。

**镜头 04**

How Algorithmic Confounding in Recommendation Systems Increases Homogeneity and Decreases Utility

Chaney et al. · RecSys 2018

Simulation study

[论文全文](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1710.11214)。示意图对应模型中的反馈与效用结果，不标成真实用户的长期实验。

**镜头 11**

Auralist: Introducing Serendipity into Music Recommendation

Zhang et al. · WSDM 2012

Liked, previously unknown artists per 20-item list

Basic Auralist 2.90 → Full Auralist 5.86

n = 21 · Music recommendation study

Proposed: user-adjustable novelty

[原文 Table 3 与 §6](https://dmice.ohsu.edu/bedricks/courses/cs606-ir/papers/zhang_2012.pdf)。两项数值是未知且喜爱艺术家的平均数量。作者也报告平均单项喜爱评分下降，因此不把这一组数字标成总体满意度或长期兴趣增长；用户调节新颖程度是作者在 §6.3 提出的设计建议。

**镜头 12**

PURS: Personalized Unexpected Recommender System for Improving User Satisfaction

Li et al. · RecSys 2020

Unexpected + relevant

[论文](https://doi.org/10.1145/3383313.3412238)。图示表达平衡意外性与相关性的设计方向，不能替代 OtherWise 自身的用户研究。
