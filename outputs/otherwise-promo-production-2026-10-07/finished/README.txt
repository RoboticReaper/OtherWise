Both films are 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, H.264/AAC, approximately 1 minute 56 seconds.

The film includes the existing Adam narration and Quiet Horizons instrumental, two existing AI animations, a newly made technology-themed feed opener, animated research graphics, actual OtherWise product demonstrations, and the closing project QR code. The narration is unchanged. Captions follow the recorded voice and use the confirmed English copy.

Studio uploading remained unavailable during this session, so the complete film was edited and exported locally. This export is the completed video; the ElevenLabs cloud timeline is not the finished timeline.
