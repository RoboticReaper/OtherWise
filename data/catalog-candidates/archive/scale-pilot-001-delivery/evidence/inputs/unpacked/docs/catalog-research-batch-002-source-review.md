## Actionable finding

**R1 — Aquatint relation provenance (`local:catalog:aquatint`).** The card's acid-resistant ground, acid biting, tonal depth, and use with line etching are supported. However, `facet_of → local:catalog:etching-printmaking` is labeled `source_asserted`. The cited Oxford museum page describes the mechanism and says aquatint is normally combined with etching; it does not explicitly make the parent/subtype assertion. Treat that classification as editorial reasoning, or add an inspected reference that directly states the family relationship. The current citation establishes a technique connection, but its acid-biting premise alone should not turn the inferred classification into a source assertion. This is a provenance finding, not a finding that the classification or card is factually false. The evidence/source locator also says “Process,” whereas the inspected heading is “Technique.” Use the actual heading for repeatability. [Oxford History of Science Museum, Aquatint, Technique](https://hsm.ox.ac.uk/aquatint).

## Relationship-type spot checks

Three positive structural examples illustrate the distinction between source support and plausible navigation:

- **`facet_of`:** NPS explicitly identifies crossdating as a procedure within dendrochronology, so the parent relationship follows from the passage, not just from similar subject matter. [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/articles/archeology-dendrochronology.htm).
- **`application_of`:** NASA explicitly says the CD nozzle chokes flow at a sonic throat, sets mass flow, and expands downstream supersonically. This directly supports a use of choked flow. [NASA Glenn](https://www.grc.nasa.gov/www/k-12/airplane/nozzled.html).
- **`broader_topic`:** the Met's express novel classification supports Genji's membership in Novels. Its bridge to ekphrasis remains editorial because the two cited sources establish separate media-transformation premises rather than an explicit Genji–ekphrasis relationship. [Met](https://www.metmuseum.org/exhibitions/listings/2019/tale-of-genji), [Academy of American Poets](https://poets.org/glossary/ekphrasis).

Aquatint is the contrasting case: the source supplies a mechanism and a combination with another process, while the particular parent classification still calls for an inference or another reference. This does not invalidate every `source_asserted` structural relation in the batch.
