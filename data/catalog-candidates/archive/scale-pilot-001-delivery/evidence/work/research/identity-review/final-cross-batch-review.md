## Rechecked earlier comparisons

- prior-01: Fail-safe track-circuit signalling / Fail-safe defaults. The shared fail-safe design principle does not make this concrete railway detection application identical to default authorization.
- prior-02: Stereographic projection / Map projection. Stereographic projection is one specific geometric pole-to-plane construction. Map projection is the wider class of mappings used to flatten Earth and negotiate cartographic distortions.
- prior-03: Mechanical resonance / Sympathetic resonance. Mechanical resonance is strong response to forcing near a natural frequency. Both are existing controls and the source-specific coupling discussion is not a second new generic-resonance count.
- prior-04: Allocation concealment / Double-blind study. Allocation concealment prevents recruiters foreseeing the next assignment before enrollment. - prior-05: Diagnostic sensitivity, Diagnostic specificity, Positive predictive value / Signal detection theory, Absolute sensory threshold. Diagnostic metrics condition results on disease status or vice versa. Signal detection models decisions amid noise; an absolute sensory threshold concerns detection probability for stimulus strength. These quantities/frameworks are not interchangeable.
- prior-06: Pure-tone audiometry / Absolute sensory threshold, Pitch (music). Pure-tone audiometry is a clinical procedure mapping detection thresholds by sound frequency. Absolute threshold is a general psychophysical property; musical pitch is the perceived high/low ordering of notes.
- prior-07: Observer's paradox in sociolinguistics / Experimenter expectancy bias, Social desirability bias. Observing/recording ordinary speech can change the speech researchers seek. Experimenter expectancy concerns researcher-side expectations affecting observation, while social desirability is participant response shaped to appear acceptable. The mechanisms can coexist without being synonyms.
- prior-08: Impression management / Social desirability bias. Impression management is shaping others’ impressions in ordinary social performances. Social-desirability bias is a measurement distortion in responses given to appear acceptable.
- prior-09: Social norms / Normative social influence, Informational social influence. A norm is a shared behavioral expectation.
