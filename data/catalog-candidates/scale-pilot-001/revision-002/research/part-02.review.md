# Part 02 meaning review

Reviewed 2026-10-08 using the Matt Pocock research workflow: inspect primary or official passages, compare the source subject with the registered inventory identity, and cite the resulting decisions. This review covers exactly the 36 IDs in `part-02.input.json`.

All 36 decisions reuse their existing registered IDs. A card's narrow example, mechanism or jurisdiction-specific explanation did not create a new whole subject. No new relationship is proposed.

The main scope checks were:

- **Midrash:** the inspected Sefaria explanation treats midrash as biblical interpretations/stories and demonstrates Bereshit Rabbah. Q202344 includes exegesis alongside classical rabbinic literature, supporting reuse of the broad subject. Corpus and separately delimited interpretive methods should still remain distinguishable. [Sefaria](https://voices.sefaria.org/sheets/179504), [Q202344](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q202344).
- **Choropleth mapping:** the card defines and explains the map type, rather than specifying a separate production workflow. [Esri documentation](https://doc.arcgis.com/en/insights/latest/create/choropleth-maps.htm).
- **Brouwer:** the same theorem identity is supported, but the imported description omits essential hypotheses. Keep the card's closed-ball self-map statement. [Theorem statement](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/BrouwerFixedPointTheorem.html).
- **Named subjects:** the Train scene, Kepler survey and XMM mirror details explain their same whole opera/spacecraft. [Composer](https://philipglass.com/compositions/einstein_on_the_beach/), [NASA](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/kepler/), [ESA](https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/XMM-Newton/XMM-Newton_factsheet).
- **Legal scope:** the cards retain their US federal context. The underlying doctrines are the existing subjects; the inspected opinion, rule and statute provide the particular formulation. [Lujan](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supct/html/90-1424.ZO.html), [Rule 56](https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/frcp/rule_56), [28 USC 2241](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/28/2241).

Remote identity limits: 34 QID HTML pages were inspected through the web tool, which exposes indexed/crawled HTML rather than verifying a current EntityData revision. Q683979 and Q281126 HTML failed; their EntityData URLs were inaccessible. Those two decisions use the supplied registered descriptions plus the inspected legal passages. The JSON records these limits per row. The Berkeley founder-effect URL timed out, so the NHGRI definition was used; the bare USGS liquefaction URL timed out, while its parameterized FAQ URL and another official USGS explanation were accessible. The Rule's legacy OSB URL returned 404; the official archived chapter text was inspected.
