## Finding007-R1: Issue preclusion

The opening’s unconditional bar omits actual litigation and the boundary on who can be bound. The later phrase “can matter” only qualifies different claims. This can misleadingly suggest that a prior decision settles the issue against any later litigant. Retain the distinction from claim preclusion, but say it can bar a party who had a full and fair opportunity to litigate an issue actually litigated and necessarily determined in a valid final judgment. Do not impose an absolute same-parties rule, because limited exceptions and nonmutual uses exist.
