Two code-review axes checked specification compliance and engineering standards before the final run. Reviewers did not inspect held-out outputs while ranking code or configurations were being frozen.

The V5-known review verified known-ID exclusion, ambiguity/explicit-meaning handling, source-preserving presentation, path focus, soft redundancy, finite bounds, deterministic seeds and isolation from evaluator grades. Twelve new application tests covered the policy. A reviewer compared the original recommendation implementations across 36 variant/kind/mode combinations; recommendations and diagnostics matched the previous frozen implementation.

Two specification clarifications were resolved before grading the corrected development run: declare the 0.35/0.50/0.60 distance-cap treatments, and make the no-content ablation change only its content weight. The plan explicitly states that input near-restatements are excluded geometrically, while other known/selected concepts use verified identity exclusions and soft similarity penalties. The V5 geometric overlap labels are diagnostic, with no hidden quota relaxation in the original variants.

A failing CLI regression demonstrated that primary-only nomination discarded a broad-track leader. `tune()` now performs separate bounded development searches and seals their union with track champions in the nomination identity. The regression passed after the fix; a follow-up reviewer verified missing-track/ineligible-grade rejection and broad-only compatibility. Specific remains the primary reporting track. Search ties preserve configuration order, whereas the descriptive scoreboard uses measured latency as a quality tiebreak; both K-connection and K-literal were consequently included.

Shortlist values, sourced examples and reported regressions match saved outputs.
